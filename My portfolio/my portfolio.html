Technical Toolkit

Python, SQL, Excel & Power BI

My technical stack combines programmatic analytics, structured database querying, spreadsheet modeling, and interactive Business Intelligence—giving me the flexibility to extract, clean, analyze, automate, and visualize data at every stage of the analytics lifecycle.

Python for Data Analysis & Automation

Writing clean, reusable scripts to automate repetitive data workflows, perform statistical analysis, batch-process files, and streamline end-to-end reporting pipelines.

SQL for Querying & Data Extraction

Writing optimized queries using joins, CTEs, subqueries, window functions, and aggregations to extract accurate, analysis-ready datasets directly from relational databases.

Excel for Data Cleaning & Management

Leveraging Pivot Tables, Power Query, XLOOKUP, conditional logic, and structured formulas for rapid cleaning, reconciliation, ad-hoc modeling, and operational spreadsheets.

Power BI for Dashboards & Reporting

Designing interactive multi-page dashboards with star-schema data models, DAX measures, dynamic slicers, and drill-through views for clear executive performance tracking.

Pandas & NumPy for Data Manipulation

Transforming high-volume tabular data with vectorized numerical computations, grouping, reshaping, merging, time-series indexing, and feature preparation at speed.

Matplotlib & Seaborn for Visualization

Building statistical plots—distributions, correlation heatmaps, trend lines, and comparative charts—to diagnose patterns during EDA and communicate insights clearly.
